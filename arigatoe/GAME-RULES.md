# Arigatoe — Player Game Rules

## The aim

Arigatoe is a rock-paper-scissors reel game. Start a round by tapping the reels. Eight player symbols then travel from the top of the reels towards the bottom, one row at a time. Win duels to keep moving; lose a duel and that player symbol leaves the round.

The default bet is **1.00 unit** per round.

## Symbols and duels

Each column starts with one player symbol: Rock, Paper, or Scissors. The player symbol is changed to a fresh random Rock, Paper, or Scissors whenever it successfully moves down to another row.

The usual rules decide each duel:

- Rock beats Scissors.
- Scissors beat Paper.
- Paper beats Rock.

On every row, the reel symbols are revealed and each active player symbol duels the symbol directly beneath it.

- When the player wins, it moves into that position and continues to the next row.
- When the player loses, it disappears for the rest of the round.
- A Wild always counts as a win and lets the player move on.
- A Scatter also lets the player move on and is added to the Scatter count.

## Reel symbols

At the start of a round, each reel position is covered. A cover can be either regular or mystery.

- **Regular covers** reveal Rock, Paper, or Scissors with equal chances.
- **Mystery covers** reveal Wild or Scatter. Wilds appear more often than Scatters.

## Ways to win

Winning duels on their own do not pay. Payouts are awarded for complete lines and Scatters.

### Vertical lines

Complete all five positions in one column and win **1.00 unit** (one times the bet).

### Horizontal lines

Complete all eight positions across one row and win the row’s displayed multiplier times the 1.00-unit bet. Each row receives its own randomly selected multiplier before the round begins.

Possible row multipliers are:

| Multiplier | Chance in the selection pool |
| --- | --- |
| x2 | Most common |
| x3 | Common |
| x5 | Less common |
| x8 | Rare |

Wilds and Scatters count as successful positions when completing either type of line.

### Scatter payouts

Scatter rewards are paid at the end of the base-game round:

| Scatters collected | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 | No Scatter reward |
| 3 | 1.00 unit + Bonus Round |
| 4 | 5.00 units + Bonus Round |
| 5 or more | 10.00 units + Bonus Round |

## Random feature

At the beginning of a base-game round, the Random feature can trigger. The Random symbol appears over the reels and sends extra Mystery covers into the game.

- It replaces regular covers only; existing Mystery covers are not changed.
- Between **3 and 10** extra Mystery covers can land on the reels.
- The extra Mystery covers remain in place for the rest of that round.
- Because Mystery covers can reveal Wilds or Scatters, the feature increases the chance of special-symbol outcomes.

## Bonus Round

Collect **three or more Scatters** during a base-game round to enter the Bonus Round. A message confirms the number of Scatters awarded; choose **Start Bonus** to continue.

The Bonus Round has **7 free spins**. The wheel is filled once at the beginning of the feature with 12 symbols:

- 4 Rocks
- 4 Papers
- 4 Scissors

These wheel symbols stay in the same positions throughout all seven free spins.

For each free spin:

1. A new player symbol—Rock, Paper, or Scissors—is chosen.
2. The wheel spins and the arrow selects one wheel position.
3. The player symbol duels the selected wheel symbol using the normal rock-paper-scissors rules.

The free spins play automatically until all seven have finished, even if a duel is lost.

### Bonus streak and multiplier

Consecutive bonus wins build the Streak Bar:

| Consecutive wins | Final multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | x2 |
| 2 | x3 |
| 3 | x5 |
| 4 | x8 |
| 5 | x10 |

- A loss before five consecutive wins resets the current streak.
- Once five consecutive wins have been reached, the x10 streak is secured for the rest of the Bonus Round.
- After free spin seven, the currently lit Streak Bar multiplier is applied to the total win from the triggering base-game round, including its Scatter reward.
- If no streak is lit at the end, no bonus multiplier is applied.

When the Bonus Round ends, the final win is shown. Closing that message returns the player to a fresh base-game board.

## Game information

The information bar shows the number of spins played, the current bet, the round win, and Return to Player (RTP). RTP is the total amount paid back divided by the total amount wagered during the session.
