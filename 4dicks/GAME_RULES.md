# Four Dicks — How to Play

Match cards diagonally, collect prizes, and discover features that can multiply your win. The game plays each round automatically after you deal.

## Start a round

Tap the game board or press **Deal cards**. Each new round costs **1.00 play unit**. You can also use Space or Enter when the board is selected.

A fresh deck of **48 cards** is created for each round. It can contain repeated ranks, plus Wilds and Scatters. The first 16 cards fill the **4×4 board**.

- **Top panel:** shows the ranks available in this round. Counters track collected pairs. The Wild counter shows your Wild multiplier.
- **Bottom panel:** shows up to 16 upcoming cards, with the next card on the left. An empty panel means there are no more cards to deal.

## Make diagonal matches

Two cards match when they have the **same rank** and touch diagonally: one row up or down and one column across. Their suits can be different. For example, a King of Hearts can match a King of Spades.

Cards next to each other horizontally or vertically do not match. Wilds match only Wilds, and Scatters match only Scatters.

The game checks the top two rows first, then the middle two, then the bottom two. Within each pair of rows, it checks diagonals down to the right before diagonals down to the left. A glowing line shows each match.

Matched cards disappear. The remaining cards slide left and up, and upcoming cards fill the spaces. Checking starts again at the top. This repeats until no diagonal matches remain—even if cards are still waiting in the deck.

## Pair prizes

These prizes apply to each collected pair at the current 1.00-unit bet:

| Matching pair | Prize |
| --- | ---: |
| 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6 | 0.05 units |
| 7, 8, 9, or 10 | 0.25 units |
| Jack, Queen, or King | 1.00 unit |
| Wilds | 0.50 units, plus a multiplier |
| Scatters | 0.50 units, plus progress toward Free Spins |

All pair prizes add together before multipliers are applied.

## Wild multipliers

Each Wild pair reveals a random multiplier: **×2, ×3, ×5, ×8, or ×10**. Smaller values appear more often.

Wild multipliers **add together**. Collecting ×2 and ×3 gives you ×5, which multiplies all your pair prizes. With no Wild pair, your prizes stay unchanged.

**Example:** 2.00 units in pair prizes with a total Wild multiplier of ×5 becomes **10.00 units**.

## Random High Cards

Before each round, there is a **10% chance** of triggering **HIGH CARDS FEATURE!**

All low cards, 2–6, become extra Jacks, Queens, or Kings. The feature adds sets of high ranks already in the deck. If none are present, it can use any of those three ranks. The deck still contains 48 cards.

## Gamble: Double or Nothing

Gamble is offered when there are **no cards left to deal and no diagonal matches left**. Unmatched cards may still remain on the board.

- Choose **Collect** to keep your current win.
- Choose **Gamble**, then pick **Hearts** or **Spades**.

After the suit shuffle, each suit has an equal **50% chance** of winning. A correct choice doubles your win. A wrong choice leaves you with **zero winnings for the round**. There is one Gamble attempt per eligible round.

## Bonus: Free Spins

Collect Scatters through diagonal matches to unlock the treasure map:

| Scatters collected during the round | Award |
| --- | ---: |
| 2 | 10 Free Spins |
| 4 | 20 Free Spins |

After the main-game matches finish, press **Continue** on the award popup. Free Spins run automatically and cost no extra bet.

Each Free Spin works like this:

1. Between **1 and 64 cells** on the 8×8 map receive random multipliers.
2. The sonar chooses a direction: top-left, top-right, bottom-left, or bottom-right. Its chosen area blinks.
3. A large **X** shrinks into a random cell.
4. Two thick lines draw a right angle from that cell. Multipliers in the area extending toward the chosen corner are collected, including the X cell if it contains a value.

If either direction points straight out of the map at an edge, only the X cell is collected. Empty cells add nothing.

The counter above the map shows Free Spins left. The counter below it adds up all collected multipliers. At the end, this total multiplies your main-game win. If you collect no multipliers, that win stays unchanged.

**Example:** a 10.00-unit main-game win and a bonus total of ×20 pay **200.00 units in total**.

If both Gamble and Free Spins are available, Gamble happens first. Free Spins multiply the amount you keep afterward; a lost Gamble therefore leaves the final payout at zero.

The final popup shows your full win. Press **Continue** to return to the main-game board.

## Your session statistics

- **Spins:** the number of paid rounds started. Free Spins do not add to this count.
- **Free Spins Hits:** the number of bonus rounds awarded.
- **Total Win:** all completed round payouts added together, before subtracting bets.
- **Return to Player:** Total Win divided by total bets, shown as a percentage. This describes your current session, not a guaranteed return.

Statistics reset when you reload the page. This prototype uses play units.
