# Space Balls: How to Play

## Your mission

Send the asteroid into the other player's goal!

You play on the **left**, with the blue goal. The computer plays on the **right**, with the pink goal.

The first player to reach **10 points** wins, but they must be **2 points ahead**.

## Pick your planet

1. Use the left and right arrows to look at the four planets: Earth, Jupiter, Lava, and Ice.
2. Pick one and press **Confirm**.
3. Tap or click the screen to start.
4. Wait for **Ready?**, **Steady!**, and **GO!**

The asteroid starts in the middle. It flies off in a different direction and at a different speed each round.

## Watch the asteroid

The asteroid is the rocky ball with a fire tail. The tail trails behind it as it flies.

The asteroid bounces off the outside edges of the court. It can cross the line in the middle.

Both goals move up and down. Each goal changes speed when it reaches the top, so watch carefully!

## Planets pull the asteroid

Every planet has a circle around it. When the asteroid enters that circle, the planet pulls it closer. This pull is called **gravity**.

The pull can bend the asteroid's path and change its speed.

- **Jupiter** has the strongest pull, but gives the smallest push.
- **Earth** has a medium pull and a medium push.
- **Ice and Lava** have weaker pulls, but give the biggest pushes.

## Use your voice

Press **Enable microphone** and allow the browser to use your microphone.

Your voice controls the planet you picked **on the left side**.

- Talk to make your planet's circle light up.
- Talk louder to give a stronger push.
- Your planet can push the asteroid only while it is inside that planet's circle.
- The push makes the asteroid go faster in the direction it is already moving.

**Tip:** Give it a push when it is flying toward the pink goal!

The game shows a message when your planet gives the asteroid a push. If the asteroid is somewhere else, your planet lights up and waits for it.

You can also hold the **Boost** button or the **Space** key to play without a microphone.

## What does the computer do?

The computer controls the other planets on both sides.

It gives pushes of different sizes when the asteroid is flying toward the other side's goal. It stays quiet when the asteroid is flying toward its own goal.

## Score points

- Hit the **pink goal** to score a point for you.
- Hit the **blue goal** and the computer scores a point.

After each goal, the asteroid returns to the middle. Wait for **Ready?**, **Steady!**, and **GO!** before the next round.

## Win the match

You need at least **10 points** and a lead of **2 points**.

- **10–8:** You win!
- **10–9:** Keep playing. You are only 1 point ahead.
- **11–9:** You win!

If the score reaches **9–9**, or a higher tie such as **10–10**, the game says **Deuce**. That means the scores are tied near the end. Keep playing until someone leads by 2 points.

**Match point** means a player can win the match by scoring the next point.

## Play again

The winner screen tells you who won and shows the final score.

Press **New Game** to go back to the planet picker. The score goes back to **0–0**, and you can choose a new planet!
