## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full-screen PNG background for the Fool House haunted-mansion bonus game.
Primary request: A complete architectural floor plan of an old mansion drawn like a classic blueprint, on aged yellowish paper lying on an attic table.
Composition: portrait 2:3 image, close overhead view looking straight down. A single large unfolded building-plan sheet occupies approximately 93% of the image; only a narrow irregular rim of dark worn attic-table wood is visible along the edges. The complete mansion plan is the main subject and fills the sheet: interconnected rooms, substantial outer walls, corridors, central hall, staircases, doors with drafting arcs, windows and architectural linework. Keep the entire overall building footprint inside the paper and clearly readable. No room labels or lettering.
Style and materials: richly textured, atmospheric gothic game background; precise traditional blue/navy drafting ink on warm pale yellow ochre parchment, fine construction lines and subtle grid. Paper is dusty, creased, stained by old coffee rings and splashes, with frayed edges, small tears, missing edge chips, and several visible aged repair-tape strips and paper patches. Fine spider webs cling across the paper corners and bridge to the table. Repairs and stains remain peripheral enough that the mansion plan is still clear.
Lighting: soft dim amber attic light, subtle shadows at the paper edges, weathered and mysterious, enough contrast to read the blue architectural lines.
Constraints: opaque edge-to-edge background. No logo, title, watermark, signatures, stamps, numbers, text, lettering, labels, UI, buttons, game reels, people, or large foreground props. No coffee cup. The plan, not the tabletop or attic room, dominates the image.
