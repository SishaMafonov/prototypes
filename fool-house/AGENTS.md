- This is an independent npm / TypeScript / Vite canvas prototype. Run commands from `fool-house/`.
- `src/game.ts` owns weighted draws, global 5+ identical regular-symbol wins, integer-cent payouts, removal/refill, collection, and Fibonacci multipliers. `src/main.ts` owns scene loading, round/stage sequencing, canvas layout/animation, input, and session UI. `src/style.css` owns the page, HUD, popup, and multiplier badge.
- Preserve the background-to-symbol contract documented in `CHANGELOG.md`. Low/mid/high sprites each have five equal horizontal frames indexed from 0 to 4. `scatter.png` is one standalone icon, drawn whole. Mystery uses canvas artwork without a sprite file.
- Keep the background full-screen, reels horizontally centered, title at the top, collection between reels and HUD, and layout responsive at high pixel densities. Center reels vertically when space permits; on short portrait phones use the full play area and shift upward to preserve readable cells without overlapping panels.
- The board is 5 columns × 5 rows (25 cells). `src/assets/reels/main-5x5.png` is the active frame; the original 5 × 4 `main.png` is preserved as source history. Cell apertures in `src/scene.ts` are measured in the active image's 1164 × 1351 source pixels.

Base paytable and door odds are unchanged from v14; bonus rewards now double on ascent, so current combined RTP is unmeasured. Read module16; module15 is historical. Floor transfers/features clear regular cells while retaining Scatters; a fresh floor resets collection/global regular indexes. Partial rooms continue while at least five regular slots remain. Read modules 11, 13 and 14.
- Mystery is a canvas-drawn pink question mark, nonpaying/non-Wild until transformed. All Mysteries on one board share one uniform current-room Low/Mid/High choice. After landing, show raw results for 500 ms, then run 1600 ms selection +450 ms reveal before regular wins after every gameplay draw/refill. The 500 ms pause also applies with reduced motion. Startup uses a static regular-only nonwinning preview; no Mystery picker or automatic sequence before the first tap. Keep input locked; animation draws no outcome RNG. Read module 14.

Continue starts the door bonus with the entire already-credited base round win. Door chances remain Coin30%, Multiplier4.73%, Stairs15%, Ghost50.27%. - Match regular symbols by both tier and icon index anywhere on the board. Remove all members of every 5+ group simultaneously, refill only holes without gravity, and re-check until no wins remain. Pay once per group based on quantity, times the active multiplier: low 5/6/7+ = 0.01/0.02/0.03; mid = 0.02/0.03/0.05; high = 0.03/0.05/0.10. Counts above seven use the seven-symbol award, while every matching symbol is removed. Money is integer cents.
- A winning current-room identity independently draws one multiplier from the exact 30-slot `ROOM_MULTIPLIERS` bag: 14×2, 8×3, 5×5, 3×8. Multiply its whole-group award by stage × room, including repeated wins of collected tiers. Show the separate `room-bonus` animation before removal/credit; never pay the base amount again. Other-room groups get room x1 and no bonus RNG draw.
- Collection persists across refills within one background. A removed matching-background group collects that tier. Collect all three to show the stage popup, clear regular cells while keeping Scatters visible, switch backgrounds, reset collection, and refill the other cells free. Keep the Scatter bonus-awarded guard across all free floors; reset it only for a new paid round. Multipliers are x2, x3, x5, …, capped at x144 for subsequent free stages; reset to x1 on a new paid round.
- Spins count paid deals and free respins, not refills. Only paid rounds add to wager. Round win includes its entire refill/free-stage chain; session RTP = total awards / paid wagers. The opening board is a fixed nonwinning regular-only preview: no board RNG, growth, Mystery, Scatter, evaluation or awards. Wait for the first tap/Enter/Space.
